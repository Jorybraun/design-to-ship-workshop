Workshop · For designers · 2–3 hours

From idea to a shipped site.

You decide what to build. The AI coding agent asks, executes, verifies, and reports. Learn six clear phases and the prompts that keep you in control.

  • Human roleDecide product, spec, design, and boundaries
  • Agent roleInterview, structure, plan, implement, verify
  • OutcomeA shipped release with evidence you can review

You are the product owner and reviewer.

Start Phase 1 → Read the guide →

Teaching only. The example DesignCraft conference site is an unapproved illustration. Open the prompts, adapt them to your own project, and approve every real action separately.
